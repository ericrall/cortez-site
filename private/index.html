Cortez Privacy Policy

Effective: November 1, 2026

Published by RALL DIGITAL LLC

1. What Cortez Does

Cortez turns your iPhone or iPad into a Bluetooth keyboard and trackpad for a computer you can see. It uses your device's camera to read the computer's screen on the device itself, and sends keyboard and pointer input to the computer over Bluetooth, the same way a hardware keyboard does.

2. Data We Collect

We do not collect personal data.

  • No accounts. Cortez does not require sign-up, login, or any account creation.
  • No analytics. The app contains no analytics or advertising SDK. We do not track usage, sessions, taps, or behavior.
  • No advertising. There are no ads and no ad tracking.
  • No data sold. We do not sell, share, or monetize any user data.
  • Nothing leaves your device. This version of Cortez sends no camera frames, screen text, commands, passwords, or contact details to any server operated by us or by anyone else.

3. Camera

Cortez uses your device's camera to view the computer screen you point it at. Camera frames are processed entirely on your device for text recognition and screen detection, and are discarded after processing. They are not stored and not transmitted.

4. Bluetooth

Cortez connects to your computer over Bluetooth Low Energy as a standard HID (Human Interface Device) keyboard and pointer. The connection is direct between your iPhone or iPad and your computer; no data passes through our servers. Only keyboard and pointer input reports are transmitted.

5. Passwords, Face ID and Touch ID

Quick login: if you choose to use Quick login, you choose one login (a password, and its username if it has one) once from iOS AutoFill, which is part of iOS and asks for Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode according to your device settings. Cortez receives only the password and any username of the login you choose and saves them on this device only, in the iOS Keychain. The password is protected by Face ID or Touch ID, and Cortez asks for Face ID or Touch ID each time it types the password; your device passcode alone does not release it. The username is kept on this device without that check, so Cortez can show it to you before you type, and anyone who can open Cortez on your unlocked device can see it or, in Two-step mode, type it. The saved login is not synced to iCloud. It is never sent to our servers or to anyone else, and leaves this device only as keystrokes to the computer you are controlling. Cortez does not write it to its logs or action history. Cortez deletes the saved login when you choose "Forget saved login" in the Quick login panel, when you choose a different login, when you use "Erase all Cortez data" in Settings > Privacy & Data, and if iOS no longer lets Cortez read it, for example after Face ID, Touch ID or the device passcode has been changed or removed (Cortez then removes it and asks you to choose it again). If you delete Cortez, iOS can keep the saved login on the device, so choose "Forget saved login" before you delete it; a leftover login is deleted the first time you open a reinstalled Cortez. Cortez does not store any other password.

Separately, you can lock Cortez itself behind Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode.

6. Face Data

Cortez does not collect, access, store, or share face data. When you unlock Cortez, iOS performs Face ID or Touch ID itself; Cortez receives only a yes/no result from Apple's LocalAuthentication framework and never sees, stores, or transmits your face geometry, images, or any biometric template. When iOS AutoFill asks for Face ID or Touch ID before giving you a password, that check happens entirely inside iOS. When you use Quick login, Cortez asks iOS to release the Quick login password from the Keychain; iOS performs the Face ID or Touch ID check itself, and Cortez receives the password only if the check succeeds. Cortez never sees any face or fingerprint data. Cortez does not use face detection, face tracking, or the TrueDepth camera. Camera frames of your computer screen are processed on-device and discarded immediately.

7. Contacts

This version of Cortez does not read your contacts.

8. Microphone, Speech, Motion, Local Network

This version of Cortez does not use the microphone or speech recognition and does not access your local network. It reads the device's motion sensor on the device only to tell when the device is being held still, so it can read the screen more accurately; motion data is not stored or transmitted.

9. On-Device Storage

Cortez stores preferences, saved devices and an action history locally, in the app's sandbox. The only password it keeps is the one login you choose to save for Quick login, in the iOS Keychain, as described under "5. Passwords, Face ID and Touch ID". This version does not sync any of this to iCloud. You can erase everything from Settings > Privacy & Data > "Erase all Cortez data", which also clears anything Cortez has placed in the iOS Keychain (iOS can keep Keychain items after an app is deleted). Deleting the app removes the rest.

10. Third-Party Services

This version of Cortez uses no third-party services and makes no network requests on your behalf. If a future version adds cloud-assisted features, this policy will be updated before those features are enabled, and they will be off by default.

11. Children

Cortez is not directed at children. You must be 18 or older to use it. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

12. Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted at https://cortez.is/private with a new effective date.

13. Contact
