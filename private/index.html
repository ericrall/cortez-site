Privacy Policy

Cortez iOS App — published by RALL DIGITAL LLC

What Cortez Does

Cortez turns your iPhone into a Bluetooth keyboard and trackpad for a computer you can see. It uses your phone's camera to read the computer's screen on the phone itself, and sends keyboard and pointer input to the computer over Bluetooth, the same way a hardware keyboard does.

Data We Collect

We do not collect personal data. Specifically:

  • No accounts. Cortez does not require sign-up, login, or any account creation.
  • No analytics. The app contains no analytics or advertising SDK. We do not track usage, sessions, taps, or behavior.
  • No advertising. There are no ads and no ad tracking.
  • No data sold. We do not sell, share, or monetize any user data.
  • Nothing leaves your phone. This version of Cortez sends no camera frames, screen text, commands, passwords, or contact details to any server operated by us or by anyone else.

Camera

Cortez uses your iPhone's camera to view the computer screen you point it at. Camera frames are processed entirely on your device for text recognition and screen detection, and are discarded after processing. They are not stored and not transmitted.

Bluetooth

Cortez connects to your computer over Bluetooth Low Energy as a standard HID (Human Interface Device) keyboard and pointer. The connection is direct between your iPhone and your computer; no data passes through our servers. Only keyboard and pointer input reports are transmitted.

Passwords and Face ID

If you save a password for Cortez to type on your computer, it is stored in the iOS Keychain on your phone and protected by Face ID or your passcode. Cortez asks for Face ID before typing a saved password and never transmits passwords anywhere except, as keystrokes, to the computer you are controlling.

Contacts

With your permission, Cortez reads your own contact card to fill your name, email, phone, or address into a form on your computer. It reads only what you ask it to fill, at the moment you ask, and never uploads your contacts.

Microphone and Speech

If a version of Cortez offers voice commands, speech is recognized on your device using Apple's speech framework. Audio is never recorded, stored, or sent to any server.

Motion and Local Network

Motion data is used only to keep the on-screen interface level as you tilt the phone. Local network access, if requested, is used only to discover an optional companion receiver on your own network; nothing is sent over the internet.

On-Device Storage

Cortez stores the following locally on your iPhone, in the app's sandbox:

  • Preferences (display name, handedness, platform choice)
  • Saved devices (Bluetooth device names and platform types)
  • Saved passwords (in the iOS Keychain, Face ID protected)
  • Action history (which actions ran and whether they succeeded)

This version does not sync any of this to iCloud. You can erase everything from Settings inside the app ("Erase all Cortez data"). Deleting the app removes everything except saved passwords, which iOS keeps in the Keychain; use "Erase all Cortez data" first if you want those gone too.

Third-Party Services

This version of Cortez uses no third-party services and makes no network requests on your behalf. If a future version adds cloud-assisted features, this policy will be updated before those features are enabled, and they will be off by default.

Children

Cortez is not directed at children. You must be 18 or older to use it. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted at this URL with a new "last updated" date.
